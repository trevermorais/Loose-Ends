Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Loose Ends does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No account is required and no sign-in exists.
  • Your progress (coins, kills, upgrades) is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party trackers and needs no internet connection.

Uninstalling the app deletes all saved progress.
